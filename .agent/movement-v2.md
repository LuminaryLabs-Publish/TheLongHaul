The following original content is retained unchanged.\n\n# The Long Haul Movement Kit V2 — invisible spring-mass

**Scope:** The Long Haul only. V1 remains byte-for-byte unchanged in `src/game/truck-kit.mjs`. `createLongHaulProductKits` selects V2 by default; `movementVersion: "v1"` is rollback. Only one truck kit is registered.

The truck/cargo effective mass already belongs to `cargoDynamics`; V2 uses a fraction of that existing mass in a single invisible 3D damped spring, rather than adding another mass or driving engine. In local body coordinates it integrates `q'' = -ω²q - 2ζω q' - a_vehicle`, where `a_vehicle` is measured from world velocity delta, not a steering or drift sign. The equal-and-opposite spring reaction is available for telemetry. The spring's displacement drives chassis lean and pitch through constrained suspension compliance. Excess COM projection and lateral inertia beyond the existing tire-support track cause rollover risk. No hardcoded drift direction forces inward lean.

The world owns drive velocity, cargo mass, freight and collision; the spring owns compliant weight transfer. Its **spring tension is fixed at the base-truck reference mass**, so added freight increases inertial deflection rather than raising spring stiffness automatically; cargo COM height increases the leverage of its reaction on chassis lean. Unstable cargo shift follows the same spring with its existing `shiftStrength`; secured cargo still reports zero shift. V1 cargo spring and scripted roll are not executed in V2. Contact remains the legacy spherical ground-support approximation: this implementation does not claim six independent tire contact forces. The existing render rig visualizes the authoritative body pose; wheel grounding is not reimplemented.

Verification gates: V1 source checksum unchanged; V2 default selected and unique; spring passivity and boundedness; 30/60/120 Hz parity; driving/braking/turning/sustained slide and countersteer; top-heavy cargo; impact and F recovery; run save/load V1 migration, pause and menu freeze; all regressions; real WebGL visual footage and audio. Browser frame inspection and target-hardware acceptance are separate from deterministic math tests. No workflow/YAML files may be touched.
