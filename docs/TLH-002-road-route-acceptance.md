# TLH-002 — Roads and route choices: implementation and acceptance record

**Owner:** LUM-000154 / TLH-002 (route gameplay). **Source baseline:** `49c0fdd04232994c06c38cf84b7e8d0a104af7f9`. **Status:** feature implementation and deterministic testing complete; full TLH-002 human/rendered acceptance remains open.

## What changed

- Generated v2 freight junctions now present **two separate roadside signs**: `WIDE LEFT` (safe) and `EXPRESS RIGHT` (narrow bonus), with correct physical roadside positions, arrow directions, warning distance and stable sign IDs. The route-choice contract records both side labels, sign IDs and warning progress, while retaining original edge and fallback identifiers.
- Generated scenic paved bypasses and gravel detours now have **side-specific warning signs** with left/right turn arrows, the fact that the main highway remains open and the side/entry/warning information in `routeChoices`. Signs are placed near the mathematically joined beginning, before the optional road physically separates.
- Curve, grade, tunnel, bridge, lane taper and construction feature signs now carry useful short guidance rather than generic “drive the next horizon” text.
- **Version-1 Endless saves retain their original one-sign layout and unchanged route-choice metadata.** The fixed v2 geometry, collision rules, cargo gates, pay/save semantics, generated runtime and source branch architecture remain unchanged.

## Validated on the local candidate

- `npm test`: **22 suites passed**. This includes **720 chunks, 2,160 continuity seams**, 12,480 road/earth-deck clearance checks and existing truck, rollover, cargo, saving, skills, ability and Campaign invariants.
- `tests/road-chunks-regression.mjs`: for 30 seeded courses, verifies freight signs point toward the correct physical route and generated bypass signs agree with actual branch sides and preserve drivable mainline alternatives; geometry and legacies still pass.
- `tests/endless-highway-regression.mjs`: 24 finite procedural seeds retain optional connected off/on-ramps, drivable eight-edge roundabout loops, signed bypass choices and real cell-owned yield markings positioned on paved ramps, with the solid center-island contract.
- `tests/road-choice-traversal-regression.mjs`: **six input-driven NexusEngine truck fixtures** covered freight safe, freight express, scenic mainline, scenic bypass, gravel mainline and gravel bypass. All physically followed the *selected* road for at least 100 simulation ticks through each route centre, reached the endpoint with no off-road ticks, had no rollover, and crossed streaming windows. These are bounded start-state fixtures, **not** an uninterrupted ten-minute gameplay film or traffic simulation.
- `npm run test:roads:traverse`: **PASS** — 600 seconds / 36,000 ticks of real engine, truck and freight simulation; 13,756.67 metres; all 10 road categories; 14 pickups and 14 deliveries across all 5 cargo profiles; 0 cargo losses; 42 window updates; at most 17 retained edges; maximum road offset 1.737 m. Independent engine/physics evidence, not browser, traffic, video or human acceptance.
- `npm run build:host` succeeded with pinned NexusEngine/Three.js runtime. Generated vendor assets are intentionally **not changed** by this road-only commit.

## Remaining acceptance and limitations

- Actual rendered browser inspection (`npm run validate:roads`) was not available in this sandbox: local Chromium was unable to load the game. Do not describe the new signs as screenshot-approved or field-tested.
- The LUM-000162 final road/sign/garage/collider asset pack, player-readable signs at full-speed/portrait, browser traffic through lane tapers, human driving and physical Mac/Safari performance remain review gates.
- This work does not change the Campaign “Coming Soon” boundary, NexusEngine, CI actions, or `.github/workflows/deploy-pages.yml`.
