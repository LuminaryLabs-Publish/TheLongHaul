Driver briefing

Deliver the freight

Endless: keep driving through loading gates and blue receivers. Cargo loss ends a contract, not your journey. Campaign retains its geographic routes and receiving yards.

DriveW or ↑ accelerates. S or ↓ brakes then reverses. A and D steer. Double-tap W or ↑ for a five-second charge, then three seconds to recharge. Brake to cancel. Space holds boost while using extra fuel. X applies the handbrake.
DeliverEndless: choose the wide left lane or the express right shortcut. Drive through to load, then reach the blue receiver before the cargo deadline. Campaign: stop at your assigned depot and press F.
NavigateM shows the next pickup or receiver. A missed pickup moves ahead. The wide route fits every truck; a clean express route builds your combo.
Protect the loadTake cyan ramps for airtime. Avoid striped barriers. F rights a rolled rig with a time and cargo cost. Recovery resets after eight seconds.
ScoreClose passes, clean landings and express loading routes build a ×5 combo. Drive clean for four seconds to bank it. A crash loses the pending combo; banked points remain. Each 20 banked points earns $1. Delivery timing still counts.
Drift & developBrake with S while steering, or hold Shift to slide. Countersteer to redirect your stored momentum; stopping loses it. Skilled driving earns XP and points. U pauses the world for three upgrade choices. Q and E activate your equipped abilities.
CameraC switches between a readable chase camera and a low hood camera. Escape pauses. R retries the same course.
